================================================================================
                          1. GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

1.1 LOGGING IN
--------------
1. Open your web browser and go to your Mesaq dashboard URL
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Login"

If you forgot your password:
- Contact an administrator to reset your password
- Administrators can reset passwords from: Members > [Member Name] > Reset Password


1.2 DASHBOARD OVERVIEW
----------------------
After logging in, you will see the main dashboard with:
- Summary statistics (total members, total balance, events, etc.)
- Quick access cards for common actions
- Recent activity feed

Admin Dashboard shows:
- Total Members
- Total Account Balance
- Upcoming Events
- Members with Outstanding Payments

Member Dashboard shows:
- Your personal payment status
- Your payment history
- Upcoming events


1.3 NAVIGATION
--------------
The sidebar on the left contains all main sections:

For Admins/Board Members:
- Dashboard       - Main overview page
- Calendar        - View events and schedule notifications
- Members         - Manage all members
- Finance         - Track finances and transactions
- Events          - Manage community events
- Documents       - Upload and share documents
- Messaging       - Send WhatsApp messages to members
- Settings        - System configuration

For Regular Members:
- Dashboard       - Personal overview
- Calendar        - View upcoming events
- Members         - View member directory
- Payments        - View your payment history
- Events          - View events
- Documents       - Access shared documents


1.4 USER ROLES & PERMISSIONS
----------------------------
The system has several user roles:

- Admin           Full access to all features
- Board           Same as Admin (Board members)
- Manager         Administrative access
- Finance Officer Access to finance features
- Logistics Officer Access to event management
- Public Officer  Access to communications
- Member          Basic access (view-only for most features)


================================================================================
                        2. MEMBER MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

2.1 VIEWING ALL MEMBERS
-----------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Members

The Members page displays all registered members with:
- Name and avatar
- Group assignment
- Phone number
- Household members count
- Role
- Payment status (PAID/UNPAID)

Sorting Options:
- Name (A-Z) - Alphabetical order
- Most Paid - Members who paid the most first
- Least Paid - Members who paid the least first
- Unpaid First - Members with outstanding balances first

To refresh the list: Click the "Refresh" button


2.2 CREATING A NEW MEMBER
-------------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Members > "+ Create" button (top right)

Steps:
1. Click the "+ Create" button
2. Fill in the member details:
   - Full Name (required)
   - Email Address (required, unique)
   - Phone Number (with country code, e.g., +61412345678)
   - Address
   - Role (select from dropdown)
   - Group (select from Group 1-5)
   - Household Members (number of family members)
   - Date Joined
3. Click "Create Member"

Note: A temporary password will be generated. Share this with the member.


2.3 VIEWING MEMBER DETAILS
--------------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Members > Click on any member row

The member detail page shows:
- Personal information (name, email, phone, address)
- Payment balance and status
- Payment history
- Membership status by month
- Event attendance history


2.4 EDITING MEMBER INFORMATION
------------------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Members > [Member Name]

On the member detail page:
1. Click "Edit" next to any editable field
2. Make your changes
3. Click "Save"

Editable fields:
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Address
- Group
- Household Members
- Date Joined


2.5 CHANGING MEMBER ROLES
-------------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Members > [Member Name]

Steps:
1. Find the "Role" section
2. Click the current role to open dropdown
3. Select the new role:
   - Member
   - Manager
   - Finance Officer
   - Logistics Officer
   - Public Officer
   - Board
   - Admin
4. Confirm the change

Note: Changing roles affects what the member can access.


2.6 SETTING GROUP LEADERS
-------------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Members > [Member Name]

Steps:
1. Open the member's detail page
2. Find the "Group Leader" option
3. Toggle ON to make them a group leader
4. The member will now appear as leader for their group


2.7 RESETTING MEMBER PASSWORDS
------------------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Members > [Member Name]

Steps:
1. Open the member's detail page
2. Scroll to the bottom
3. Click "Reset Password"
4. Confirm the action
5. A new temporary password will be generated
6. Share this password with the member securely


2.8 MEMBER PAYMENT STATUS
-------------------------
Each member has a payment balance calculated as:
- Expected Payments: Monthly fee × Months since joining
- Actual Payments: Sum of all membership payments
- Balance: Actual Payments - Expected Payments

Balance Status:
- Positive (Green): Member has paid in advance
- Zero: Member is up to date
- Negative (Red): Member has outstanding payments


================================================================================
                        3. FINANCE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

3.1 FINANCE OVERVIEW
--------------------
Navigation: Sidebar > Finance

The Finance page shows:
- Current account balance
- Bank account tabs (switch between accounts)
- Monthly transaction view
- Search functionality
- Quick actions (upload statement, add transaction)


3.2 BANK ACCOUNTS
-----------------
Creating a New Bank Account:
Navigation: Finance > "Add Account" button

Steps:
1. Click "Add Account"
2. Enter Account Name (e.g., "Main Account", "Donations")
3. Enter Account Number (6 digits)
4. Enter BSB (format: XXX-XXX)
5. Check "Donation account" if applicable (donations only, no member matching)
6. Click "Add Account"

Switching Between Accounts:
- Click on the account tabs at the top of the Finance page

Deleting an Account:
1. Select the account to delete
2. Click "Delete Account"
3. Type "confirm" to verify
4. Click "Delete Account"

Note: You cannot delete the last remaining account.


3.3 UPLOADING BANK STATEMENTS
-----------------------------
Navigation: Finance > "Upload Bank Statement" button

Supported Format: PDF bank statements

Steps:
1. Click "Upload Bank Statement"
2. Select your PDF file
3. Wait for processing (may take a minute)
4. Transactions will be automatically imported
5. Review imported transactions

The system will:
- Extract transaction dates, amounts, and descriptions
- Attempt to match transactions to members
- Categorize transactions based on keywords


3.4 MANUAL TRANSACTIONS
-----------------------
Navigation: Finance > "Add Transaction" dropdown > "Regular Transaction"

Steps:
1. Click "Add Transaction"
2. Select "Regular Transaction"
3. Fill in the details:
   - Date
   - Type (Credit/Debit)
   - Category (Membership Payment, Event Payment, Donation)
   - Transaction Name
   - Description (optional)
   - Amount
   - Match to Member (optional)
4. Click "Add Transaction"


3.5 MATCHING PAYMENTS TO MEMBERS
--------------------------------
Automatic Matching:
When uploading bank statements, the system attempts to match payments
to members based on:
- Banking name in transaction description
- Member name
- Keywords

Manual Matching:
Navigation: Finance > Click on "Unknown" in Member column

Steps:
1. In the transactions table, find the Member column
2. Click on the current member name or "Unknown"
3. Search for the member by name, email, or phone
4. Click on the correct member to match
5. The payment will be linked to that member


3.6 TRANSACTION CATEGORIES
--------------------------
Available Categories:
- Membership Payment - Monthly membership fees
- Event Payment - Payments for specific events
- Donation - Charitable donations

Changing Category:
1. In the transactions table, find the Category column
2. Click the dropdown
3. Select the new category
4. The change is saved automatically


3.7 ADVANCE PAYMENTS
--------------------
For members paying multiple months in advance:
Navigation: Finance > "Add Transaction" > "Advance Payment"

Steps:
1. Click "Add Transaction"
2. Select "Advance Payment"
3. Search and select the member
4. Enter number of months (1-12)
5. Add description (optional)
6. Click "Record Payment"

This will:
- Create payment records for future months
- Update member's balance accordingly


3.8 MAIN MEMBERSHIP PAYMENT ACCOUNT
-----------------------------------
The main membership payment account is used for:
- Payment reminder messages (BSB and account number in messages)
- Default account for membership payments

Setting the Main Account:
Navigation: Finance > Select an account > "Set as Main" button

Steps:
1. Click on the account tab you want to set as main
2. Click "Set as Main" button
3. The account will be marked with a star icon

Note: Only one account can be the main membership account.


3.9 ADJUSTING BALANCE
---------------------
To manually adjust the account balance:
Navigation: Finance > Click on the balance amount

Steps:
1. Click on the large balance number
2. Enter the new balance
3. Click the checkmark
4. Enter a reason for the adjustment
5. Click "Confirm Adjustment"

An adjustment transaction will be recorded.

                      12. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQs
================================================================================

12.1 COMMON ISSUES
------------------

ISSUE: Cannot log in
SOLUTION:
1. Check your email is correct
2. Check caps lock is off
3. Try resetting your password (contact admin)
4. Clear browser cache and cookies

ISSUE: Page not loading / showing errors
SOLUTION:
1. Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Check your internet connection

ISSUE: Transactions not importing from bank statement
SOLUTION:
1. Ensure the PDF is from a supported bank
2. Try uploading a smaller date range
3. Check if the PDF is not password protected
4. Contact support for assistance

ISSUE: WhatsApp messages not sending
SOLUTION:
1. Check if Picky Assist API is configured
2. Verify the member has a valid phone number
3. Check if test mode is enabled (messages go to test number)
4. Review the console logs for errors

ISSUE: Member not showing correct payment status
SOLUTION:
1. Check if payments are matched to the correct member
2. Verify the payment category is "Membership Payment"
3. Check the member's join date (affects expected payments)
4. Refresh the members list

ISSUE: Cannot delete a bank account
SOLUTION:
- You cannot delete the last remaining account
- Create another account first, then delete

ISSUE: Finance page shows wrong transactions when changing months
SOLUTION:
- This race condition has been fixed
- If still occurring, refresh the page


12.2 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-------------------------------

Q: How do I add a new member?
A: Sidebar > Members > Click "+ Create" > Fill in details > Click "Create Member"

Q: How do I change the monthly membership fee?
A: Sidebar > Settings > Community Settings > Membership Fee > Enter new amount > Update

Q: How do I send a message to all members?
A: Sidebar > Messaging > Check "Select All" > Type message > Click "Send"

Q: How do I export member data to Excel?
A: Sidebar > Settings > Community Settings > Export Data > Select "Members" > Choose "Excel" > Export

Q: How do I mark an event as completed?
A: Sidebar > Events > Click on event > Click "Complete Event" > Mark attendance > Complete

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Sidebar > Settings > Personal Settings > Change Password section

Q: How do I reset another member's password?
A: Sidebar > Members > Click member name > Scroll down > Click "Reset Password"

Q: How do I add bank transactions manually?
A: Sidebar > Finance > Click "Add Transaction" > Select "Regular Transaction" > Fill details

Q: How do I match a payment to a member?
A: Sidebar > Finance > Find the transaction > Click member column > Search and select member

Q: How do I schedule a notification to all members?
A: Sidebar > Calendar > Click "Schedule Notification" or click a date > Fill details > Schedule

Q: How do I create a backup?
A: Sidebar > Settings > Community Settings > Backup & Restore > Click "Create Backup"

Q: How do I set which bank account is used for payment reminders?
A: Sidebar > Finance > Select the account > Click "Set as Main" button

Q: How do I assign a group to organize an event?
A: When creating/editing an event > Select "Organizing Group" > Choose a group

Q: What does the star on a bank account mean?
A: It indicates the "Main Membership Payment Account" - used in payment reminder messages

Q: How do I view incoming WhatsApp messages?
A: Sidebar > Messaging > Click "Incoming Messages" tab

Q: How do I check if messages are in test mode?
A: Check if PICKY_ASSIST_TEST_MODE=true in environment variables

Q: Can regular members see other members' payment information?
A: No, regular members can only see their own payment history

Q: How do I generate an annual report?
A: Export member and transaction data, then use the Board Member Report generator
